3.Conclusion
In this study, we synthesized carbazole-based thiuram disul-
ﬁdes(CTDs),highlightingtheirextraordinaryreactivityforrapid
reshuﬄingundervisiblelightirradiationandunderscoringtheir
critical role as iniferters of photopolymerization. Taking advan-
tageofthesedynamicbonds,wesuccessfullyobtainedavisible-
light-induced living polymer network. This unique network
showcases a notable light-responsive characteristic that facili-
tatesfastreconﬁgurationuponexposuretovisiblelight,thereby
enabling photoinduced shape-shifting and multiple rounds of
healing with retained mechanical properties. In addition, utiliz-
ing CTDs as a visible-light iniferter, the LIL polymer network
can undergo chain growth with nBA, providing a robust plat-
form for the modulation of the network’s mechanical proper-
ties on demand. While our investigation primarily focuses on
the reconﬁguration and chain growth with only one monomer
of the LIL polymer network, it is essential to underline the
broader reactivity of the thiuram disulﬁdes, encompassing ther-
mal dissociation,[54–55] C(sp2)-S coupling,[56] and antibacterial
eﬀects.[57] These aspects suggest a vast potential for spawning a
diversearrayofinnovativethiurampolymernetworksexhibiting
“living” behavior in response to various environmental stimuli.
Consequently, we posit that further exploration into the reactiv-
ityofdiﬀerentdynamicbondswithinpolymernetworkswillun-
doubtedly pave the way for unveiling new applications for light-
induced living polymer networks with adaptive multifunctional
properties.
